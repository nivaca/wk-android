<p>Wiktionary Reader is a small app for reading the English Wiktionary. Type a word, in any language, and it shows the whole entry: etymology, pronunciation, definitions with their quotations, inflection tables, derived terms, translations and the rest.</p>

<ul>
<li>Suggestions from Wiktionary as you type, and your recent words.</li>
<li>A bar at the bottom with Back, Forward, Contents (the languages and sections of the page) and Find in page.</li>
<li>Quotations and inflection tables can be switched off from the menu; select any text in an entry to look it up.</li>
<li>A bar with the languages of the page. Give the ones you want first (codes or names: <i>la, es, Ancient Greek</i>), and hide the others if you like.</li>
<li>Section titles open and close when you touch them; wide tables scroll sideways in their own box.</li>
<li>Links to other entries open in the app, at the right language; Back returns to the previous word, where you left it.</li>
<li>Light and dark mode, with Wiktionary's own colours for the inflection tables.</li>
<li>From any app: select a word and choose <i>Look up in Wiktionary</i>, or share a text with the app.</li>
<li>Pages are kept for a week, so a word seen again opens at once.</li>
</ul>

<p>The app uses only the Android framework, with no trackers and no other libraries. The content of the entries comes from Wiktionary and its contributors, under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.</p>
